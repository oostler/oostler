Chord Quality Training
Develop your “vocabulary of recognized sounds” — hear a chord and immediately feel its quality.

Beato Method: Major = M3+m3 stacked (bright). Minor = m3+M3 (darker). Diminished = m3+m3 (tense). Augmented = M3+M3 (eerie, floating). Listen for the stacking of thirds.

Scale Degree Training
A cadence establishes the key — then identify which scale degree you hear (Beato’s tonal method).
Score: 0/0 🔥 0
Beato Movable-Do Method: Do always = the tonic. After hearing the cadence, sing from the mystery note down (or up) to Do. The distance you travel tells you the degree. Pre-dominant contains degree 4 (Fa). Dominant contains 5 & 7. Tonic contains 1 (Do).
