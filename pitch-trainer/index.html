Chord Quality Training
Develop your "vocabulary of recognized sounds" — hear a chord and immediately feel its quality.
Score: 0/0 🔥 0
Beato Method: A major triad = major 3rd stacked on minor 3rd (bright, open). A minor triad = minor 3rd stacked on major 3rd (darker, introspective). A diminished = two minor thirds stacked (tense, unstable). An augmented = two major thirds (eerie, floating). Listen for the stacking of thirds.

Scale Degree Training
Beato's tonal method: a cadence establishes the key, then identify which scale degree you hear.
Score: 0/0 🔥 0
Beato Method (Movable Do): Do always = the tonic. Hear the cadence, feel the key center, then sing from the note down (or up) to Do. The distance you travel tells you the degree. Pre-dominant chords contain degree 4 (Fa). Dominant contains degrees 5 and 7. Tonic contains degree 1 (Do).

Note Recognition
Beato's 12-reference-song method: each of the 12 chromatic pitches is anchored to a song you know deeply.
Score: 0/0 🔥 0
Rick Beato's 12-Reference-Song Method: For each of the 12 chromatic notes, memorize one song where that pitch is the very first note. When you hear an unfamiliar pitch, cycle through your references until one matches. This is not perfect pitch — but it provides a fast practical pitch-naming system for adults.
